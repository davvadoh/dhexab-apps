Core features

From arrival to a well-managed check-out.

Arrivo gives you better control of visitors without adding unnecessary work at reception. Check-ins, notifications, history and evacuation information are brought together in one system.

Check-in and check-out

Visitors check in and out on a tablet or touch screen. Group check-in for up to 20 people makes it easy to register larger groups.

Host notifications

Notify employees via email, Microsoft Teams or webhook when a visitor arrives. Notification settings can be configured for each host.

Overview and history

See who is currently on site, search previous visits, and export history to Excel or CSV.

Multiple entrances

Create separate entrances with their own kiosk URLs, for example for reception, warehouse or production areas.

Evacuation list

Generate a list of everyone on site, print it to a network printer or open it as a PDF when needed.

Customization

Customize the kiosk with your logo, colors, languages, content and themes to match your company branding.

Local hosting

Your server. Your information. Your control.

Arrivo is not a cloud service. It runs locally on your own equipment, such as a preconfigured Raspberry Pi from DHEX AB, a Synology or Unraid NAS with Docker, a Linux server or a Windows computer. Core functions work without internet access.

Who Arrivo is for

  • Manufacturing companies and warehouses
  • Offices with reception desks or visitor areas
  • Facilities with multiple entrances
  • Businesses looking to avoid subscriptions and cloud dependency

What you need

A tablet or touch screen at each entrance, a local network and an optional network printer for evacuation lists. Internet access is only needed for features such as email and Teams notifications.

Installation

Arrivo can be supplied as a ready-to-use device with the software preinstalled, or as software for self-hosting. For self-hosting, Arrivo is installed using Docker on a compatible server or NAS. Contact us for installation instructions and technical requirements.

Secure local operation

Visitor data stays on your own local network. Arrivo supports backup and restore, configurable retention periods and automatic anonymization to support GDPR compliance.

Frequently asked questions

What businesses usually want to know first.

Does Arrivo work without internet access?

Yes. Core check-in, check-out and local administration work without internet access. Email and Microsoft Teams notifications require a connection to those services.

Does Arrivo have monthly fees?

No. Arrivo is sold with a one-time licence. Pricing is provided on request, and six months of service and support are included.

Can we use several entrances?

Yes. Each entrance can have its own kiosk URL, letting you distinguish between reception, warehouse and production areas.

Can Arrivo notify our hosts?

Yes. Arrivo supports email, Microsoft Teams and webhook notifications. Settings can be configured for each host.

Can we export visit history?

Yes. Visit history can be exported to Excel and CSV. You can also filter the information before exporting.

Can we customize the appearance?

Yes. Add your logo, choose colors and languages, and customize the kiosk welcome screen.

Where is visitor data stored?

Your visitor data is stored locally on your own equipment. You can also configure retention and anonymization of older visit records to match your internal routines and GDPR obligations.

Is Arrivo an ID06 system or certified fire safety system?

No. Arrivo is a visitor management system. Its evacuation list complements your existing fire safety procedures and does not replace certified safety systems.
