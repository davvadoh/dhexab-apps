Frequently asked questions

What to know about Arrivo.

What is Arrivo?

Arrivo is a visitor management system for businesses to organize guest registration, check-in and check-out, and visitor overview.

Is Arrivo cloud-based?

Arrivo is designed for local hosting on your own equipment. Contact us for installation details and technical requirements.

Can Arrivo help with evacuation?

Arrivo supports information about registered visitors. Optional evacuation functionality and integrations depend on your setup.

How much does Arrivo cost?

Arrivo is intended to use one-time licensing rather than a traditional recurring license. Contact DHEX AB for pricing and terms.

Is Arrivo suitable for small businesses?

Yes. Arrivo is intended for companies seeking clear and simple visitor management. We can help determine whether it fits your needs.
